About

I lead SAP asset-management modernization for the Pennsylvania Turnpike Commission, a statewide toll-road authority. Most recently I led the EAM workstream of a system conversion from ECC 6.0 to S/4HANA, moving maintenance from reactive work toward data-driven asset lifecycle management, and a fresh SAP Service and Asset Manager rollout for field crews.

My work runs end to end: fit-to-standard workshops, functional design, data migration, integration, test strategy, cutover, hypercare and adoption in the field. I keep the core clean and spend time with technicians, supervisors and planners so the system is one they trust.

ECC 6.0 to S/4HANA EAM system conversion

Context
An on-premise conversion of SAP Plant Maintenance (ECC 6.0 EHP8) to S/4HANA Enterprise Asset Management. Live in 2026.
What I did
Led the EAM workstream with a cross-functional client and integrator team. Converted tens of thousands of technical objects (equipment and functional locations) with classifications, maintenance plans, task lists, notifications and orders, and upgraded Linear Asset Management for roadway assets. Validated migrated data field by field, reviewed and approved custom-code remediation against clean-core principles, set a data-archiving strategy, and ran cutover of open orders, maintenance calls and material reservations through mock cutovers.
Outcome
Maintenance and facilities operations moved to S/4HANA with dozens of interfaces intact and business running as usual soon after go-live.

SAP Service and Asset Manager on SAP BTP

Context
SAP Work Manager was reaching end of support, and field crews needed reliable mobile work execution, often offline.
What I did
Led a fresh implementation on SAP BTP: finalized requirements after as-is workshops, designed personas, work-order and notification flows, offline synchronization and client customizing, and ran testing and a train-the-trainer program. This built on earlier work leading the Work Manager 6.4 to 6.5 upgrade and its move from on-premise to BTP.
Outcome
Rolled out to hundreds of field users, with post-go-live field visits to confirm adoption and prioritize enhancements.

Usage-based fleet maintenance

Context
Fleet vehicles needed service by mileage, but low-use vehicles still needed regular attention.
What I did
Mileage readings from operational data feeds post to SAP measuring-point counters. Multiple counter plans then trigger on mileage or six months, whichever comes first. Alongside this, single-cycle and strategy plans (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual) schedule generators and building inspections, with call horizon and scheduling period set in the master-data standard.
Outcome
Preventive work is generated automatically from real usage, and technicians record defect type and cause through catalog profiles for better failure data.
